How Payday OS works

  1. Friday: your check lands. Payday OS pays the bills, savings and debt on paper first.
  2. Every day: the big green number is what you can spend out today. That's it.
  3. Spend less and some rolls to the weekend and some gets banked. Spend more and the rest of the week shrinks.
  4. Tap Month to see every day, Budget for the plan and advice.
